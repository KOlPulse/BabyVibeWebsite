The Legend of $VIBECOIN

In the vast meme cosmos of Solana, Vibing Cat emerged as pure web chaos on August 17, 2025, launched anonymously on Anoncoin.it. From day one, the dev left a single cryptic clue: only one “followed by” — Sir Doge of the Coin. The token sat quietly for a month until an anonymous comment lit the fuse: “I think it’s Elon’s friend… but that’s just my guess.”

"Did the Doge launch the Cat? Is Elon aware… or involved? The clues keep dropping."

Deeper Clues & Dev Signals

The Vibecoin banner was the only manually uploaded one on Anoncoin (others AI-generated). Hidden inside: “Sidehorlined catter” — an anagram for “Elon is the Creator ID”.

Early on, Sir Doge directly asked the community for supply and received 5.69M VIBECOIN (still fully held today). Choosing Sir Doge as the first followed-by feels intentional — a strategic nod that the dev might be working with or signaling key Doge ecosystem players.

Silent dev buybacks started hitting (first one: 5K shortly after the initial pump), backed by ongoing creator fee mechanisms. Zero words, all action.

The Great Orange Wave

This rare soft coordination amplified the project at a small market cap and turned the ultimate Doge-Cat irony into reality.
